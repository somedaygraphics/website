Raíssa Camargo —Rai, like the word “high”— is a multi disciplinary artist based in São Paulo, Brazil. She’s been making things for over 17 years and freelancing since 2024, mostly for people who make music. Posters, merch, record sleeves… the things you want to own a physical copy of.

Her design studio is Someday Graphics, but these days she mostly works under her own name. So if you know her as @somedaygraphics, you’re in the right place!

Her clients include Marcus Veltri, Ben Barnes, Rum Jungle, Riley Roth, JVK, with the occasional detour outside music, like a poster for the City of Los Angeles celebrating the Griffith Park Merry-Go-Round’s 100th birthday and restoration campaign.

As a singer and music fan herself, she loves getting lost in the world of an album or a song, and figuring out the visuals that go perfectly with it.

As a lifelong fangirl, she knows exactly what fans love and want from an artist, and she brings that sensibility to every design project.

Rai is self-taught in design, with a background in international English language teaching and assessment. Outside of work, she loves writing poetry, watching films & tv, going down research rabbit holes. She shares her home with her two cats, London and Selina.
