privacy, in plain words

last updated 17 november 2026

this is my personal portfolio. i don’t sell anything about you, i don’t run ads, and there are no tracking cookies. here’s exactly what happens with your information when you visit.

who’s responsible

me, Raissa Camargo (Rai), a freelance graphic designer based in São Paulo, Brazil, also working as Someday Graphics. you can reach me any time at rai@raissacamargo.com.

what i collect, and why

  • when you use the hire form: your name, email and whatever you tell me about your project. it’s sent to my inbox through a form service called Web3Forms, so i can reply and talk about working together. i keep these emails as long as we’re in touch or working on a project, and for my business records after that.
  • when you sign the guestbook: the drawing you make, the name you give and your note. they’re stored with Supabase, and i get an email letting me know. nothing appears on the site until i approve it; if i do, your drawing, name and note are shown publicly on the fridge. ones i don’t approve are deleted.
  • visitor statistics: i use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to see things like which pages are visited, roughly which country visitors are in, what device or browser they use and which site sent them here. it doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t follow you across other websites and doesn’t tell me who you are. the data is stored in the EU.

cookies & storage

this site doesn’t set any tracking or advertising cookies, so there’s no cookie banner. your browser does keep a couple of tiny notes just so the site works (like which gallery tab you came from). they never leave your device.

a few things load from other services when you use them:

  • my playlist only loads Spotify when you press “load the spotify player”. Spotify may then set its own cookies.
  • videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, which doesn’t set cookies until you press play.
  • the fonts come from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security.

your rights

under Brazil’s LGPD, and the GDPR if you’re in the UK or EU, you can ask me to see, correct or delete anything i hold about you, including taking your drawing off the fridge. just email rai@raissacamargo.com and i’ll sort it out as soon as i can. you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Brazil, that’s the ANPD).

i never sell or share your details for marketing, and you won’t be added to any mailing list. ♡
